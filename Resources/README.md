# Bundled assets

- `Fonts/Ndot-Regular.otf`, `Fonts/LatteraMonoLL.otf` — Nothing's typefaces, from nothing.tech.
- `Images/smart-dial.jpg`, `Images/case-buds.png` — CMF Buds Pro 2 product images, from nothing.tech.

These are owned by Nothing Technology Limited and are just used here for the app's look.
The app also runs without them, falling back to a drawn dot-matrix font and a drawn case.
